Individual project • In progress

The mathematics of lift

To what extent can fluid dynamics and mathematical modelling explain the generation of lift in modern aircraft?

An individual research project looking at the fluid dynamics behind lift, and how well mathematical models describe what actually happens on a modern aircraft wing.

Side project

How Light Travels

An exploration of the physics and mathematics behind light, written from a lighting designer's perspective. It runs from Maxwell's equations through Snell's law and the Fresnel lens to the inverse square law, which I tested with lux meter readings and a Python model.
