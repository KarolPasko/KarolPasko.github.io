Moving Wash Light

Moving wash lights bring full-colour washes, smooth pan and tilt, and adjustable beam spread to your production. They light stages, performers and audiences, add movement and atmosphere, and can be fully programmed from any DMX lighting console. They suit concerts, theatre, corporate events and parties.

Moving Spot Light

Use them to pick out performers, project patterns and textures, or sweep beams through haze for big aerial looks. They can be fully programmed from any DMX lighting console and suit concerts, theatre, corporate events and parties.

Chauvet Data Stream 4 DMX Splitter

The Chauvet Data Stream 4 is an optically isolated DMX splitter that takes one DMX input and sends it out on four separate outputs. Each output has both 3-pin and 5-pin XLR connectors. It boosts and protects your control signal, so a fault on one line won’t take down the rest of the rig. That makes it ideal for splitting DMX to different areas of a stage or venue. A thru port lets you link additional units for larger systems.

Available to hire in Blackpool, the Fylde Coast and Lancashire.

LED Par Lights

LED par lights deliver bright, even washes of colour for stages, uplighting and venue décor. With full RGBW colour mixing, smooth dimming, and built-in strobe and colour effects, they’re a versatile workhorse for any production. They run cool and draw little power. They can be controlled from a DMX lighting console or run standalone in auto and sound-active modes, so they suit everything from concerts and theatre to weddings, parties and corporate events.

Available to hire in Blackpool, the Fylde Coast and Lancashire.

LED Battens

LED battens are 1-metre bars of light that give smooth, even colour across a wide area. They’re ideal for cyc and backdrop washes, uplighting walls, edging stages and set pieces, and footlights. With full colour mixing, smooth dimming, and pixel or segment control on many models, they can create chases and effects along the length of the bar. They link end to end for longer runs and can be controlled from a DMX lighting console or run standalone in auto and sound active modes.

Available to hire in Blackpool, the Fylde Coast and Lancashire.

Haze Machines

Haze machines fill the air with a fine, even mist that makes light beams visible without the thick clouds of a smoke machine. They bring out the full effect of moving lights, lasers and beams, adding depth and atmosphere to any stage or venue. Output can be set manually, on a timer, or by DMX from your lighting console, giving you a consistent, long-lasting haze throughout the show. They suit concerts, theatre, clubs and corporate events.

Available to hire in Blackpool, the Fylde Coast and Lancashire.

Cold Spark Machines

Cold spark machines create stunning fountains of sparks without the heat, smoke or flame of traditional pyrotechnics. Unlike real pyro, the sparks are cool to the touch, so they’re safe for indoor use and close to performers and guests. They’re ideal for first dances, grand entrances, stage reveals, award moments and concert highlights. Spark height and timing can be set manually, by remote, or by DMX from your lighting console, so effects fire exactly on cue.

Available to hire in Blackpool, the Fylde Coast and Lancashire.

CO2 Style Vertical Jet

Vertical fog jets fire powerful columns of fog straight up into the air, lit from below by built-in LEDs for a striking, CO2-style blast effect without the gas cylinders. With full colour mixing, they can match any show’s colour scheme. They’re perfect for drops, entrances, dancefloor moments and stage highlights. Bursts can be triggered by remote, timer or DMX from your lighting console, so each blast hits right on cue.

Available to hire in Blackpool, the Fylde Coast and Lancashire.

Avolites T1

The Avolites T1 is a compact USB dongle that turns a Windows laptop or PC into a full Avolites Titan lighting console. It gives you a universe of DMX output and the same professional Titan software used on Avolites’ larger desks, including the fixture library, palettes, cue lists and shape effects. It’s ideal for smaller shows, pre-programming, busking, or a lightweight backup to a full console, and it fits in your pocket.

Available to hire in Blackpool, the Fylde Coast and Lancashire.

Akai APC Mini

The Akai APC mini is a compact USB MIDI controller with an 8x8 grid of backlit pads and nine faders. It plugs straight into a laptop with no extra power needed and can be mapped to lighting software to give you hands-on control: fire cues, palettes and effects from the pads, and use the faders for dimmers, playbacks or the grand master. It’s a great way to add physical buttons and faders to a laptop-based lighting setup such as Avolites Titan.

Available to hire in Blackpool, the Fylde Coast and Lancashire.

4 Way Artnet Node

A 4-way Art-Net node converts lighting data sent over a standard Ethernet network into four separate DMX outputs. That lets one network cable from your console or laptop feed up to four universes of fixtures around the stage or venue. It’s ideal for larger rigs, long cable runs, or anywhere you want to distribute control from front of house over a single network line instead of multiple DMX runs. Many nodes also support sACN, so they work with most modern consoles and lighting software.

Available to hire in Blackpool, the Fylde Coast and Lancashire.
