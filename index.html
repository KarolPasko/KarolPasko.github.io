About

Hi, I'm Karol.

I'm a freelance lighting and sound technician and the founder of Karol Pasko Technical. I combine technical know-how with creativity and calm problem solving to bring productions to life.

I've worked across theatre, festivals, concerts, school productions and live events – adapting quickly to new venues, equipment and teams, and delivering under pressure.

Whether you need a designer, a programmer, an operator or an extra pair of experienced hands, I'm happy to help.
