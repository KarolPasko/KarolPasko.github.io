What I offer

Services

Lighting is what I do. Hire me for a single show, a full production run or ongoing lighting support.

Lighting Design

Designs for theatre, musicals, concerts and events, from plots and paperwork through focus to opening night.

Programming & Operation

Showfile building, cueing, effects and busking, ideally on Avolites Titan, and also on grandMA and ChamSys.

Rigging & Focus

Hanging, cabling, patching and focusing fixtures, so the rig is safe and ready for programming.

Technical Management

Planning the lighting side of a production, sourcing equipment, working with suppliers, and keeping it on time and on budget.

Lighting Training

Teaching students and staff how to rig, program and run a lighting desk safely and confidently.

Show-Day Crew

An extra lighting tech for load-ins, focus and operation, who adapts quickly to new venues and kit.

Need sound or video too? Lighting is my main service, but I can rig sound and video equipment as part of a production, and I can put you in touch with trusted colleagues for sound and video design and operation.

About

Hi, I'm Karol.

I'm a lighting designer and technician based in Blackpool, working across the Fylde Coast and the North West.

Through Karol Pasko Technical I design, program and operate lighting for theatre, concerts, school productions and live events, on my own or as part of a larger technical team. My desk of choice is the Avolites Titan, and I'm equally at home on grandMA and ChamSys.

Lighting is where I focus. I can rig sound and video when a production needs it, and for anything more I'll put you in touch with colleagues I trust. Alongside the work I study Mathematics, Further Mathematics and Computer Science at Lancaster University School of Mathematics.
